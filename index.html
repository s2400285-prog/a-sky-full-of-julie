02 / A note in the clouds

Letters written above the clouds.

For the things I sometimes forget to say, and the things I hope you always know.

Touch the blue flower.

Personal correspondence

My dearest Baby Julie,

I’m grateful that you came into my life. I find myself carrying small pieces of our time together into the rest of my day. A message from you can make me smile long after I’ve put my phone down. Sometimes I remember the way you said something ordinary and wish I could hear you say it again. Those are the moments I’m glad I paid attention to.

Gratias tibi ago. Thank you for letting me get to know you at your own pace. I want you to feel appreciated in the everyday ways that matter. I want to remember what you tell me, ask how the things you care about are going, and make room for you to feel exactly how you feel. You can come to me on the quiet and difficult days, too.

I hope we keep learning how to understand each other. When I miss what you’re trying to say, I want to slow down and ask. When I get something wrong, I want to own it and do better. I want to support your dreams in the ways you actually need, even on days when the kindest thing I can offer is patience and a listening ear.

I love writing beautiful words for you. I also want you to recognize my love when there’s no letter to open. I hope you’ll feel it when I keep a promise, when I follow up on something you told me, and when I make the time to be there. I want to keep choosing you in those small ways, day after day.

Ad astra per aspera, through hardships to the stars. If life takes us through a difficult season, I pray God gives us the wisdom to speak gently and the courage to grow. I trust Him with the timing of our story. What I ask for is a heart that can love you well in the chapter we’re living now.

I’m so glad I get to know you, Julie. I hope you open this letter again on an ordinary afternoon and feel, even for a moment, how happy I am that you’re here.

With all my love, James

06 / First light

Until the sky
runs out of blue.

I tucked one last secret into the first light.

Baby Julie,

If I could leave one thing written across the sky, it would be the simple truth that I am grateful for you.

I do not know every chapter that awaits us, nor can I promise that every day will be effortless. But I hope to meet each tomorrow with a heart willing to understand you, a spirit willing to grow, and a love that never forgets to appreciate you.

You are not merely a beautiful part of my days. You have become one of the reasons those days feel beautiful.

And if I were given countless skies to explore, I would still find myself searching for the one where I get to see you.

In perpetuum, Baby Julie.

With all my love,
James Navares Traigo.
