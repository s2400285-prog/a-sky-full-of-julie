# A Sky That Learned to Love

A blue-sky storybook for Baby Julie, made with love by James Neil Traigo. The sealed invitation leads to six chapters, from a first look at the sky to the last letter at sunrise.

## Open the site

1. Extract the ZIP and open the `julie-love-sky` folder in Visual Studio Code.
2. Copy your personal media into the folders below. Use the exact filenames and lowercase extensions.
3. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**. Select **Open Your Sky** to begin.

The story runs as a static site with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. It needs no package installation, build step, or external fonts. If you are using another local static server, serve the project folder as its root.

| Your file | Project path |
| --- | --- |
| First JPEG photo | `assets/images/julie1.jpg` |
| Second JPEG photo | `assets/images/julie2.jpg` |
| Personal MP4 video | `assets/videos/our-moment.mp4` |
| Personal MP3 song | `assets/music/our-song.mp3` |

If you add or replace media while the site is open, refresh the page. Export iPhone HEIC photos as JPEG and use MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio for broad playback support. The photo viewer keeps the source proportions and shows each full image. The blue scrapbook mounts and film illustration stay visible when a personal file is unavailable.

## Explore the story

- Use the chapter bar on desktop or the numbered chapter menu on a small screen. The browser’s Back and Forward buttons also work.
- Open the cloud and star in the first chapter. There’s a little heart near the edge of the orbit and a final secret in the sunrise illustration.
- The letter types itself as the paper comes into view. Select **Read the whole letter** to jump to the complete text, or select **Replay the handwriting** to see it again.
- Open either scrapbook photo for its caption. Use the lightbox arrows or the left and right arrow keys to switch photos, and Escape to close.
- Select the film’s play button for native video controls. The **Our song** dock opens the soundtrack controls, including seek and volume. Music begins when you press play and continues as you change chapters.
- Touch all five stars to read the promises. At sunrise, choose **Keep This Little Sky Forever** to see the ending. The story follows your device’s reduced-motion preference.
